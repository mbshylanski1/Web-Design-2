The Girl Behind the Lens

My name is Madeleine Shylanski, and I am a St. Louis-based photographer. I am a recent graduate of Columbia College with a degree in English and minors in marketing and graphic design. I have proficient skills in digital photography, design and editing. I started using a camera my sophomore year of high school and since then, I’ve been able to use my photography for art projects, internship work and most importantly, you! I am always up for a challenge, and photography has transformed the way I see even life’s simplest things. My work is authentic and celebratory, and I want every photo to represent someone the way they want to be seen by the world.
